Subject: Paylane support agent — what is ready and what we need before launch

Hi Paylane team,

The agent can help customers with payment questions and refund eligible payments up to $75 once they confirm their email and card’s last four digits. It checks the payment’s owner, age and status, and prevents a second refund of the same payment.

A person takes over for larger or older payments, frozen cards, legal action, suspected fraud and account closure. The agent cannot unfreeze a card, close an account or promise when money will arrive. It won’t share an email or full card number, and it only confirms a refund or support ticket after the system records it.

This version uses made-up customers and mock APIs. Before launch, I’d need your real sign-in and payment integrations, the source of card-freeze reasons, and a support queue with an owner and agreed response times. Please also confirm whether partial refunds are allowed, how to count the 30-day window, and how you want unverified urgent cases and smaller fraud reports handled.

I recommend starting with a small supervised group, reviewing the conversations and handoffs together, and expanding after the launch checks pass.
